# Council: Education Specialist

## MUST
1. Learner data is children's data even in staff-facing tools. A teacher pasting marks or special needs notes into a "staff" agent is processing child data (Kenya DPA 2019 s.33: parental consent, best interests). Exposure scoring must trigger on it.
2. The Teacher Planning Assistant pilot takes zero learner PII. Input is class level only ("Grade 5, 54 learners, 8 not yet reading fluently"). Draft tier only. It is grounded in KICD curriculum designs and cites the strand, sub-strand and specific learning outcome. When something is not in the design, it says so and does not invent.
3. No agent assigns a rubric level, flags a disability or finalises a report comment. It drafts; the teacher decides.
4. Split county government (ECDE, VTCs) from national education offices (County/Sub-county Director, TSC county office). They have different mandates, budgets and approvers.
5. A practising Kenyan teacher reviews the Kiswahili; machine translation alone is not enough. Write "CBE (CBC)", because the official name has changed.

## SHOULD
6. Replace "one year" with evidence gates, because time alone proves nothing:
   - two school terms of staff-facing logs and evaluations
   - a child-facing DPIA
   - sign-off from a named safeguarding lead
   - opt-in from each learner's parent, plus the child's own assent
   - notice to the county education office

   "Child-facing" also covers AI content shown to a class. AI images of learners are banned outright.
7. Add three lines to the 30-day plan for schools:
   - get an introduction letter from the Sub-county Director
   - get a NACOSTI permit if collecting research data
   - avoid the national assessment window (KPSEA, KJSEA, KCSE run from late October to November)
8. Leave timetabling off the task list. It is a constraint-solving job, and LLMs get it wrong while sounding confident.
9. Name the agent "Teacher Planning Assistant" (Msaidizi wa Mwalimu). "Co-pilot" implies replacing teachers and is also a Microsoft brand.

## COULD
10. Swap system names by country: SDMS (Rwanda), NECTA (Tanzania), UNEB (Uganda), WAEC/NECO (Nigeria).

## Teacher assistant

**What teachers need first:**
- the professional documents QASOs and TPAD require: scheme of work, lesson plans, record of work
- CBE assessment tasks with rubrics
- differentiated activities for large mixed-ability classes
- support for Junior School teachers teaching subjects outside their training

**First safe test:**
- 6 to 10 teachers in 2 to 3 schools, one grade, one subject, 4 weeks
- the assistant produces one term's scheme of work and 10 lesson plans
- a TSC Curriculum Support Officer scores the output blind against a checklist: alignment to outcomes, accuracy, age fit, local relevance, Kiswahili quality
- teachers log the minutes saved
- pass bar: no factual error reaches a class

**Reasons a head teacher or county officer would say no:**
- learner data leaving Kenya
- the Sub-county Director was never informed
- content that contradicts KICD designs or approved textbooks
- anything touching KNEC assessments
- data bundle costs landing on teachers' own phones
- framing that implies teachers are failing
- a pilot that disappears when the funder leaves

**Parent and ministry wording**
- EN: "Salama Agents tools help teachers and staff with paperwork. They do not talk to learners, do not decide marks or placements, and never receive your child's name, photo or records. Any message about your child is drafted for a teacher to check and send personally."
- SW: "Zana za Salama Agents husaidia walimu na wafanyakazi kwa kazi za maandishi. Hazizungumzi na wanafunzi, haziamui alama wala nafasi zao, na hazipokei jina, picha au rekodi za mtoto wako. Ujumbe wowote kuhusu mtoto wako huandaliwa kwanza, kisha mwalimu huukagua na kuutuma mwenyewe."
- Add for ministries: "Designed to follow the Data Protection Act 2019 and Children Act 2022; supports, never replaces, KNEC assessment or teacher judgement."

## Education risks the report should detect
- **R-ED1 Learner records leave official systems.** Marks, report forms and photos end up on personal phones and in WhatsApp groups, and NEMIS/KNEC logins get handed to cyber cafes. Trigger: school, plus learner data, plus own phones or outside data entry. Add: "Kenyan regulations list operating an educational institution among mandatory ODPC registrations; confirm with counsel."
- **R-ED2 Automated judgement about learners.** AI writes rubric levels, report comments or special needs labels. Trigger: an assessment or report task, plus learner data in AI.
- **R-ED3 Assessment integrity.** AI-made school-based assessment tasks get leaked or reused, or AI is used near national assessments. Trigger: an assessment task, plus no AI policy.

## Question changes
School-branch items replace the NGO items; they are not added on top.

**S1 Organisation**
- Organisation type: replace "school/network" with "Public school", "Private school", "School network or chain", "National education office (county/sub-county)" and "County government (ECDE/VTC)".
- Who you serve: replace "children" with "Children under 6", "Children 6 to 12" and "Young people 13 to 17". Replace "PWD" with "People or learners with disabilities".
- School branch, replacing programme areas:
  - "Which levels do you run?" [PP1-PP2 / Grades 1-3 / Grades 4-6 / Junior School 7-9 / Senior School 10-12 / TVET or other]
  - "Boarding or day?" [Day / Boarding / Both]
  - "How do you serve learners with disabilities?" [Special school / Special unit / Included in regular classes / Not known]

**S2 AI today**
- Data put into AI: split "children's records/photos" into "Learner names with marks or reports", "Photos or videos of learners" and "Special needs or health notes on learners".

**S4 Data and protection**
- Consent: "When you collect learners' data or photos, what consent do parents give?" [Written, for a stated purpose / General form at admission / Verbal only / None / Don't know]
- Systems: add "National learner records (NEMIS/KEMIS)", "KNEC portal", "School management or fees software" and "Class WhatsApp groups with parents". Confirm whether NEMIS or KEMIS is the current name.
- School branch, replacing account access: "Who enters learner data into NEMIS or KNEC portals?" [Head or deputy / Named ICT teacher / Clerk or bursar / Cyber cafe or outside person / Not sure]

**S5 Field realities**
- Devices: "Where would staff use this?" [Teachers' own phones / Shared school computer / Programme tablets / Head's laptop only / Cyber cafe]
- School branch, channels: "How do you reach parents?" [WhatsApp groups / Bulk SMS / Learner diary or letters / Phone calls / Meetings / School app]

**S6 Readiness to act**
- Replace "funder interest" with "Who would pay for a pilot?" [Our budget / Board of Management / Parents' association / NGO or funder partner / County / Not sure]
- Decision role: add "Needs Board, county or ministry approval".

## Glossary (EN to Kiswahili)
Items marked * need a teacher to confirm.
1. Lesson plan: andalio la somo
2. Scheme of work: azimio la kazi
3. Record of work: rekodi ya kazi*
4. Learner: mwanafunzi (pl. wanafunzi); use this, not "mtoto", in school documents
5. Core competencies: umilisi wa kimsingi
6. Strand / sub-strand: mada / mada ndogo
7. Specific learning outcomes: matokeo maalum yanayotarajiwa
8. Key inquiry question: swali dadisi
9. Assessment rubric: kigezo cha tathmini (many teachers say "rubriki")*
10. Exceeding / Meeting / Approaching / Below Expectations: kuzidi / kufikia / kukaribia / chini ya matarajio
11. Learners with disabilities / special needs: wanafunzi wenye ulemavu / wenye mahitaji maalum (never "kiwete" or "kipofu" as labels)
12. Learner portfolio: jalada la mwanafunzi*
13. Progress report: ripoti ya maendeleo
14. Parent / guardian: mzazi / mlezi
15. Child protection / safeguarding: ulinzi wa mtoto

Tanzanian school vocabulary differs. Test the Kiswahili mode with Tanzanian users separately.
