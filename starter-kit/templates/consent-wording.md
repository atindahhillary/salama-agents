# Consent wording for forms

Purpose-specific lines to add to registration and enrolment forms (SAFE-A AN-2). Adapt to your country's law and confirm with a qualified adviser. The age at which a parent or guardian must consent differs by country; if unsure, use the strictest rule. The Kiswahili lines need review by a fluent speaker before use.

## Core statement (English)
We collect [list the information] about [you / your child] so that we can [purpose]. We use software from [providers, for example "a survey tool and an AI writing assistant"] to help our staff with [task]. Names and contact details are removed before any AI tool is used. We keep this information for [period] and then delete it. You can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time by contacting [name, phone].

- [ ] I agree to [organisation] using this information for the purpose above.
- [ ] I agree to photos of [me / my child] being used in [reports / social media]. (Separate, optional.)

## Kiswahili (beta, needs review)
Tunakusanya [orodha ya taarifa] kuhusu [wewe / mtoto wako] ili tuweze [madhumuni]. Tunatumia programu za [watoa huduma] kuwasaidia wafanyakazi wetu katika [kazi]. Majina na taarifa za mawasiliano huondolewa kabla zana yoyote ya AI haijatumika. Tunahifadhi taarifa hizi kwa [muda] kisha tunazifuta. Unaweza kuomba kuziona, kuzisahihisha au kuzifuta wakati wowote kwa kuwasiliana na [jina, simu].

- [ ] Ninakubali [shirika] kutumia taarifa hizi kwa madhumuni yaliyotajwa.
- [ ] Ninakubali picha za [mimi / mtoto wangu] kutumika katika [ripoti / mitandao ya kijamii]. (Tofauti, si lazima.)

## Rules
- Never bundle photo consent with service access.
- Record who gave consent, when, in which language, and which version of the wording.
- Read the wording aloud in the person's language where literacy is a barrier, and record that you did.
