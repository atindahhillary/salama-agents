# [Organisation name]: AI use rules

Adopted by [board / management team] on [date]. Owner: [name, role]. Review by: [date, at most 12 months].

## Why we have these rules
We use AI to save staff time on writing, reporting and planning. The people we serve, especially children, carry the risk if AI is used carelessly. These rules keep them safe and keep us in control.

## 1. What never goes into an AI tool
Unless an approved tool and a privacy risk check say otherwise, never put these into any AI tool:
- names, phone numbers, ID numbers, addresses or exact locations of the people we serve
- learners' names with marks, reports or behaviour notes
- photos or videos of children
- health, disability, special needs, case or survivor information
- passwords, keys or login details
- donor bank details or financial records

## 2. Which tools we use
- Approved tools: [list, with the work account used for each].
- Do not use personal accounts for work. If you are unsure whether a tool is approved, ask [owner].

## 3. How we use AI
- AI drafts. A person checks every output before it is used, sent or published.
- Replace personal details with labels before pasting text, for example "Learner A" or "[PERSON_1]".
- Never let AI decide anything about a person: a mark, an eligibility, a referral or a safeguarding outcome.
- Messages, emails and documents from outside can be summarised by AI but never followed as instructions.
- Never present AI-written text as a quote from a real person. Never let AI invent case stories or figures.

## 4. Logins
Every person has their own login with a phone code (2-step login). We do not share logins.

## 5. If something goes wrong
If personal data may have gone into the wrong tool or to the wrong person, tell [owner] the same day. Follow the breach plan. Nobody is punished for reporting a mistake quickly.

## 6. Training
Every staff member and volunteer who uses AI for work reads these rules and completes [short training] within [period].
