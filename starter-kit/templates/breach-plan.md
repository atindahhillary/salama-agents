# Breach plan (one page)

For when personal data is lost, leaked, sent to the wrong person or put into the wrong tool (SAFE-A EV-3). Not legal advice: notification rules and deadlines differ by country, so confirm them for each country you work in and write them below.

**Decision maker:** ______  **Deputy:** ______  **Data protection lead:** ______  **Last tested:** ______

## In the first hour
1. Whoever notices tells the decision maker straight away, by phone. Nobody is punished for reporting quickly.
2. Stop the leak: change passwords, revoke access, pause the agent (kill switch: ______), recall the message if possible.
3. Write down what happened, when, which data, how many people. Keep evidence. Do not delete logs.

## Within the deadline
4. Decide whether there is a real risk of harm to the people affected.
5. If yes, notify the regulator within the deadline that applies, and tell the people affected in plain language, in their language.

6. If we are a processor for another organisation, tell that organisation first, within the time our contract sets.
7. If children or survivors are affected, involve the safeguarding lead before contacting anyone.

## After
8. Write a short incident note: cause, people affected, actions taken, what changes.
9. Update the job card, the AI use rules or the training. Review this plan.
